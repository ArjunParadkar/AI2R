   writes it ("untrained → order ≤ 2 at δ = 0.06") is known in advance to fail. The rule registered here is the one that replicated, exactly
   as coded in `whiteboxtopo/symmetry_finite.py`: **σ is accepted iff mean|D_σ − D| < 0.25 · δ_rand** (δ_rand = mean defect of 10 random
   relabellings), candidates from the three-anchor search with two all-anchor refinements; **the group statistic is whether a FULL n-CYCLE
   is among the accepted candidates.** Sparse permutations pass any mean rule (3,406 on a memoriser), so accepted-set size is NOT a group
   order and is not used. G is reported as the cyclic group generated by the accepted full cycle.

   C_clean − C_mem = 0.22, below the 0.25 criterion.)
