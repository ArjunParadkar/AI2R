The project in one page

“Black box” names seven different failures: we do not know what a network learned (P1), why it decided (P2), when bias entered (P3), what it does not know (P4), where it will fail (P5), whether it memorised or understood (P6), or how its representations formed (P7). WHITEBOX spent five months trying to put all seven on one mathematical object — and changing the object each time the evidence demanded it.

Part I · May – July 2026

The topological film

Persistent homology of activations across training as a single instrument for all seven problems.

4 May 2026

Formulation: seven problems, one film

Duration ~4 h (est.)
Record: draft “v3”, 4 May 18:59
Research activities

Defined the target: the black box as seven distinct problems, P1–P7. Proposed that the persistent homology of activation point clouds, tracked across training epochs — the topological film — reads structure that the loss curve cannot. Wrote five hypotheses: H1 a structural phase transition invisible to loss; H2 concept crystallisation ordered by difficulty; H3 bias appears in topology before it appears in outputs; H4 a memorisation signature; H5 cross-modal universality.

Methods & tools

Three metrics on persistence diagrams Dt = {(bi, di)}: Structural Velocity SV(t) = W2(Dt, Dt−1); Topological Complexity TC = Σi(di − bi)²; Feature Persistence Lifetime. Vietoris–Rips filtration (Ripser), Wasserstein distances (persim), PCA to 30 dimensions fitted at epoch 0.

Process notes

The seven-problem framing was chosen because no existing method treats the seven failures as one object. TC was chosen because squaring lifetimes weights long-lived features — a choice that would matter in August.

Stage outcomes

A written framework and a draft paper (“Opening the Black Box of Neural Network Learning Through Persistent Homology of Activation Dynamics”).

Adjustments

Next: implement, and test every hypothesis on a vision and a language model.

Session 6 May ~22:20 → 7 May 00:20
Record: source, logs, five draft PDFs
Research activities

Wrote the core (whitebox.py) and three experiments — vision, language, memorisation — with a watchdog to chain them. Generated the first results report and four figures; produced conference-style drafts the same night and a revised draft on 14 May.

Stage outcomes

Process notes

The language model had to be shrunk to fit the session, so H3 and H5 rested on an 11 M-parameter stand-in. The cross-seed order of crystallisation was uncorrelated (ρ = −0.007) even though difficulty ordering held — recorded, not explained.

Adjustments

Decided to scale every hypothesis to real GPT-2 and to add an intervention: detection without intervention is incomplete.

Part II · 28 – 30 August 2026

The controls arrive

Three days in which the project’s best-known result was shown to be an artefact — and the method that governs everything afterwards was adopted.

28 – 29 August 2026

Library build with proper nulls

Session 28 Aug 22:58 → 29 Aug 02:12 commits
~12 h agent time · 3 h review (est.)
Research activities

Rebuilt WHITEBOX as a Python library, whitebox-topo: audit and skeleton; fast topology at scale; a calibrated memorisation signal; model-vs-data correspondence; validation batteries.

Methods & tools

giotto-ph with GPU distance matrices; bootstrap confidence intervals; feature-shuffle and label-permutation nulls; Representation Topology Divergence for correspondence; GPT-2 124M → XL benchmarks; a 31-model calibration battery (21 CNN, 10 LM) trained with graded label noise. Claude Code as execution agent.

Process notes

Feature-shuffled GPT-2 activations kept 77–86% of raw TC: the statistic was measuring how spread out the activations were, not their loops. Landmark sparsification preserved TC ordering but destroyed β1 (shipped, unused).

Stage outcomes
The 2,900× collapse was an artefact. Scrambled-label models never fitted the noise; their activations simply contracted, and TC(λX) = λ² TC(X). Models that genuinely memorise show no collapse.

What survived: a per-family signal (leave-one-seed-out AUC 0.98 CNN / 1.00 LM, converged checkpoints only) whose sign flips between CNNs and LMs. All three correspondence hypotheses refuted. P4 dies under a label-permutation null and loses to softmax confidence per sample (0.54 vs 0.90). P1 does not replicate on full-data models. P5 null.

Adjustments

Treated the collapse as the project’s founding event rather than a setback. From here on every claim is pre-registered and paired with the null that would kill it. The v1 headlines were retired with their mechanisms written down.

8 – 9 September 2026

The within-network tautology: K/N = 1

Duration ~3 h (est.)
Record: 51 commits; pause docs 0–3
Research activities

Reviewed the Tier-0 report: symmetry backends verified to 10−14; 3,648 networks trained (86% converged); all minimal explanations enumerated; the orbit partition returned K/N = 1.0 everywhere.

Process notes

A result that is identical in every cell is a statement about the test. For generic θ the stabiliser StabG(θ) is trivial, so two sub-circuits of the same network are never gauge-related: the test could not have returned anything else. The planted “N = 2K” control had been swapped out because recombined duplicate circuits are all valid — which is itself the finding.

Stage outcomes
Proposition. Explanation multiplicity has two sources: coordinate multiplicity across parameterisations (gauge) and structural redundancy within one (not gauge). Uniqueness must therefore be tested across seeds.
Adjustments

Primary metric amended to cross-seed circuit alignment; K/N = 1 kept as a documented finding.

Process notes

The first session died at 00:56 when its terminal tab closed; resumed 01:07 and every long job was moved to a detached unit. Launching too many jobs at once (load average 76–189, 30 GB RAM) got one killed for memory — fixed with capped BLAS threads and sequential queues. Stage outcomes

PASS P2: path-product attribution equals integrated gradients at r = 1.0000 on 100% of inputs; margin CV 0.07–0.10. PASS speed: open() 4.6 s per layer. PASS/FAIL P1 on Z113: 5/5 learned frequencies at r = 0.91–0.996 with the group action supplied; unsupervised Rips fails (70–207 unnamed classes, best r ≈ 0.2). PASS P3 detection: shortcut flagged within 0–4% of training on 5 seeds, 0 false alarms in 205 clean and decoy checkpoints. FAIL P4 everywhere — on GPT-2 below chance (0.40 vs softmax 0.92). FAIL P5; FAIL P6 by the letter.

The pullback metric is the right metric for cohomology. A ring RNN’s hidden state is only ~50% heading; in Euclidean distance it is a thick blob (coordinate r = 0.75–0.80). Under the g-geodesic distance the same cloud is one clean circle, 17–20× longer-lived than any other class, tracking heading at r = 0.98–0.99 — exactly the network’s own read-out accuracy. The metric quotients out what the output does not read (post-hoc).

The specification’s ground truth was wrong, and the network said why. Answer-fibres {(a, c−a)} were expected to be circles. They are intervals: the model is commutative, so within a fibre each frequency carries eika + eik(c−a) = 2eikc/2 cos k(a − c/2), a standing wave. The fibre is S1/ℤ2. Axis ratio 0.016–0.025 in the fibre, 0.996–0.999 on an unfolded slice.

The evasion result. A penalty derived from the detected circle destroyed the circle — and the shortcut re-encoded itself in a non-circular form. Three consecutive checkpoints: zero detected classes, shortcut reliance 1.000. A monitor of a representation’s shape, used as a training signal, can be satisfied by a re-encoding that leaves the function unchanged.
Adjustments

“Geodesic closure cannot be faked” is false at a late layer: a metric derived from the output makes “near” mean “same output”. Wrote the Run 2 brief to attack each diagnosed cause.
